E-commerce Store Shipping Policy

1. Standard Delivery
Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days after an order is dispatched.

2. Express Delivery
Express delivery takes 1–2 business days where this service is available.

3. Delivery Charges
Shipping charges depend on the delivery location and the selected shipping service. The exact fee is shown at checkout.

4. Order Tracking
Customers can track dispatched orders using the tracking information provided by the store.

5. Delays
Delivery may take longer because of weather, public holidays, or courier service disruptions. Customers should contact support if their order is significantly delayed.

Important: This is sample policy content for development and testing, not a verified real store policy.
