E-commerce Store Return Policy

1. Return Window
Customers can request a return within 7 days of receiving their order.

2. Product Condition
Products must be unused and returned in their original packaging. Products that are damaged by the customer may not qualify for a return.

3. Refund Processing
Once a returned product is received and inspected, approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days.

4. Non-Returnable Products
Personal-care products and customized items cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged or defective.

5. How to Request a Return
Customers should contact customer support with their order number and reason for the return.

Important: This is sample policy content for development and testing, not a verified real store policy.
